# Republic of Velmora — Passport of a Mountain Guide

A program that draws one open passport spread of the Republic of Velmora, a
country that is not there: a data page with portrait and machine-readable
zone, and a visa page whose stamps tell two journeys — over the Sarn Pass on
foot in 2022, and across the lake to Sorrenne in 2024. Every date agrees:
the passport was issued in Ostrev on 14 March 2021, and no stamp predates it.
